What is Unit 3?

WPH13 is a written practical exam — you don't carry out experiments on the day. Questions test your ability to plan experiments, process and present data, identify errors, suggest improvements, and draw conclusions. Contexts may be unfamiliar — it's the skills being assessed, not memorised procedure.
